# Macula AssetCare RFP Summary

## Offering
Macula AssetCare is positioned as an outsourced hospital asset-management department for small and mid-sized hospitals. The service combines asset-management software with on-site support.

## Core scope

- Initial digital asset-register onboarding, physical identification, tagging, document capture, location hierarchy, custodian mapping, and reconciliation.
- Asset lifecycle tracking from procurement through installation, allocation, maintenance, transfer, depreciation, replacement, and disposal.
- Warranty, AMC/CMC, preventive-maintenance, calibration, insurance, inspection, transfer, age, and end-of-life alerts.
- Asset valuation: acquisition value, accumulated depreciation, current book value, asset age, useful life, and replacement needs.
- Monthly self-audits for missing, shifted, damaged, non-functional, overdue, newly purchased, and disposal-candidate assets.
- Quarterly major audits reconciling physical assets, the register, department, custodian, maintenance, and financial records.
- Management dashboard for counts, values, categories, maintenance status, due dates, missing assets, and replacement planning.

## Commercial model

- One-time onboarding fee based on asset volume or hospital size.
- Annual managed service combining software licence, asset-management service, periodic on-site support, audit, and reporting.
- Suggested tiers: up to 50 beds, 51-100 beds, 101-200 beds, and 200+ beds.
- Additional chargeable services: complete physical audits, asset valuation, biomedical equipment audits, and large-scale asset tagging.

## Positioning

Macula AssetCare should be presented as an outsourced Asset Management Department, not primarily as another software product. Suggested promise: “We manage your hospital assets, while you manage patient care.”
